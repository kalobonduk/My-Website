Pixel Art — Creative Hobby

About This Project

Pixel art is one of my creative hobbies, where I enjoy turning ideas into small, detailed digital artworks using Aseprite.

I became interested in pixel art because of its connection to retro games, digital creativity, and visual storytelling. Working with a limited canvas and individual pixels challenges me to think carefully about composition, colors, shapes, and details.

Beyond being a hobby, pixel art gives me an opportunity to explore creativity outside my academic and professional work while continuing to learn a different form of digital design.
