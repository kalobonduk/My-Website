About This Project

As part of my Computer & Business Applications (CBA) course, I developed practical skills in using Microsoft Excel for business operations, data management, and decision-making.

The course focused on how Excel can be applied to day-to-day business activities, including organizing and analyzing data, creating reports, managing databases, and supporting data-driven decision-making.

Through hands-on work, I gained experience with Pivot Tables, Slicers, database creation, data analysis, reporting, and advanced Excel functions, helping me understand how spreadsheet tools can turn raw business data into useful insights.
